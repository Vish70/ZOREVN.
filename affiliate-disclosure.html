Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: October 9, 2026

ZOREVN believes in being transparent about how our website operates. This page explains our affiliate relationships and how we may earn commissions when visitors discover and purchase products through our links.

1. What Is ZOREVN?

ZOREVN is an affiliate product discovery website featuring selected fashion and technology products. We help visitors explore product options and provide links to external retailers where products may be available for purchase.

Our website focuses on product discovery. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, purchases are completed directly on the retailer's website, not on ZOREVN.

2. What Are Affiliate Links?

Some links on ZOREVN are affiliate links. These links may contain tracking information that allows an affiliate programme to identify that a visitor came from our website.

If you click an eligible affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase, we may receive a commission from the relevant affiliate programme, subject to that programme's terms and conditions.

3. Amazon Associates Disclosure

ZOREVN participates in the Amazon Associates programme. This programme allows websites to earn commissions by linking to eligible products on Amazon.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

A qualifying purchase does not guarantee that a commission will be earned. Commissions depend on the applicable programme rules and transaction eligibility.

4. Does Using an Affiliate Link Cost You More?

Using an affiliate link does not itself add an affiliate fee to your purchase. The price and final purchase conditions are determined by the retailer.

Product prices, discounts, delivery charges and availability may change. Always review the final details shown on the retailer's website before completing your purchase.

5. Product Selection and Recommendations

We aim to make product discovery easier by presenting a considered selection of fashion and technology finds. The presence of an affiliate link does not guarantee that a product is suitable for every visitor.

Affiliate participation should be understood as a way our website may earn revenue. It is not a guarantee of a product's quality, performance or suitability.

6. Product Information and Availability

Product images, names, descriptions, ratings, sizes and availability information on ZOREVN may change or become outdated.

Please check the relevant retailer's current product listing for the latest information before purchasing. The retailer's listing and applicable terms govern the transaction.

7. Purchases and Customer Support

When you follow an affiliate link, you may be redirected to Amazon or another retailer to complete your purchase.

The retailer handles its own checkout, payments, order processing, delivery, cancellations, returns and refunds under its applicable policies. For assistance with an order, please contact the retailer where the purchase was made.

8. Third-Party Websites

External retailers operate independently of ZOREVN. Their websites may use cookies or similar technologies to process transactions, measure referrals or administer affiliate programmes.

We do not control the privacy policies, terms or practices of external websites. Please review the relevant retailer's policies before providing personal information or making a purchase.

9. Our Commitment to Transparency

We want visitors to understand that affiliate links may support the operation of ZOREVN. We encourage visitors to review product details carefully and make their own informed purchasing decisions.

For more information about how website information is handled, please read our Privacy Policy.

10. Updates to This Disclosure

We may update this Affiliate Disclosure if our website or affiliate relationships change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about our affiliate relationships or this disclosure, please visit the Contact Us section of our website.
