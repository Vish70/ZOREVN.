Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

At ZOREVN, we value transparency and respect your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you browse our website, contact us or visit external shopping websites through our links.

1. About ZOREVN

ZOREVN is an affiliate product discovery website featuring selected fashion and technology products. Our website helps visitors explore products and access external retailer websites through product links.

We do not require visitors to create an account to browse our product collections.

2. Information We Collect

You can browse our website without directly providing personal information. If you use our Contact Us form, you may submit information such as your name, email address, subject and message.

This information is provided voluntarily and is used to understand and respond to your enquiry.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our contact form may be used to:

  • Respond to your questions and enquiries.
  • Review feedback about our website and products.
  • Communicate with you regarding your message.
  • Help prevent spam and misuse of our contact form.

We do not sell the personal information you submit through our contact form.

4. Contact Form and Email Processing

Our Contact Us form uses a third-party form processing service to deliver messages to our email inbox. Information submitted through the form may be processed by that service to provide its functionality.

The service may handle submitted information according to its own privacy policy and terms. Please review the relevant provider's policies if you would like to know more about its data practices.

5. Affiliate Links and Amazon

ZOREVN participates in affiliate marketing programmes, including the Amazon Associates programme. Some product links on our website may be affiliate links.

When you click an affiliate link, the retailer or affiliate programme may use cookies or similar technologies to attribute qualifying purchases to the referral.

If you visit Amazon or another retailer through a link on our website, that external website may collect and process information under its own privacy policy. We do not control its data collection practices.

For details about commissions and our affiliate relationship, please read our Affiliate Disclosure.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

ZOREVN's own website features may change over time. Third-party services and external shopping websites may use cookies or similar technologies for functions such as remembering preferences, measuring referrals or supporting their services.

Cookie use depends on the services and features active on the website or the external website you visit. You can review your browser settings to manage cookies. Blocking certain cookies may affect some third-party website features.

7. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to Amazon and other external websites. These websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies, terms and security practices.

Before submitting personal information or making a purchase on an external website, we recommend reviewing its applicable privacy policy.

8. Data Retention and Security

Contact messages may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries and maintain relevant correspondence.

We take reasonable care when handling information received through our website. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may choose not to submit personal information through our contact form. You can also manage cookie settings through your browser and review the privacy controls offered by external websites you visit.

If you have a question about information you submitted to us, please contact us through the Contact Us section.

10. Children's Privacy

ZOREVN is not specifically directed at children. Please contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information through our contact form so that the matter can be reviewed.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information submitted through our website is handled, please visit our Contact Us section.
